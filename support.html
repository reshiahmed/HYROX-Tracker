About the app

HYROX Tracker times each section of a HYROX race — runs, workout stations and transitions — on your iPhone and Apple Watch. It records your total time, keeps a history of past races, and lets you build custom race plans.

Everything is stored on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?
No. The iPhone app works fully on its own. The watch app is optional and adds live heart rate on your wrist.
Do I have to allow Health access?
No. All timing features work with Health permission denied. Granting it lets the app show live heart rate and save your race to Apple Health as a workout.
The timer kept running after I closed the app — is that right?
Yes. Times are calculated from real clock timestamps, so closing or relaunching the app never loses or distorts your race time.
My iPhone and Apple Watch show different times.
Both devices sync automatically when in range. Make sure they are paired, both apps are open, and Bluetooth or Wi-Fi is available. Sync resumes on its own once the devices reconnect.
How do I delete my data?
Delete individual races from the history screen, or delete the app to remove all locally stored data. Workouts already saved to Apple Health are managed in the Health app.
Can I change or disable roxzones?
Yes, in Settings. Roxzones are interleaved between work sections and can be turned off.
